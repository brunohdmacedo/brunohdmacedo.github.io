## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am a **Physical Engineer**, graduated from the Federal University for Latin American Integration (UNILA), and currently pursuing a **Master's degree in Applied Physics (PPGFISA/UNILA)** with a CNPq scholarship.

My academic and research interests focus on computational physics, theoretical investigation of materials, and the development of computational tools for scientific applications.

I work with first-principles methods, especially **Density Functional Theory (DFT)**, and explore the application of Machine Learning and Data Science to materials discovery and physical systems.

My goal is to integrate physics, mathematics, computation, and artificial intelligence to develop scientific solutions and contribute to technological innovation.

## 🔬 Research Interests

- **Computational Physics:** First-principles calculations and computational modeling.
- **Density Functional Theory (DFT):** Structural, electronic, and thermodynamic properties of materials.
- **Materials Science:** Metal hydrides, hydrogen storage, defects, and materials for energy applications.
- **Machine Learning & Deep Learning:** Data-driven approaches for scientific problems and materials discovery.
- **Data Science:** Data processing, analysis, visualization, and scientific automation.
- **Scientific Computing:** Development of computational tools and reproducible research workflows.

## 🧪 Featured Project — Bruma DFT

<div align="center">

<a href="https://brunohdmacedo.tech/bruma/">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Bruma%20DFT-Scientific%20Visualization-00A6D6?style=for-the-badge" alt="Bruma DFT"/>
</a>

</div>

**Bruma DFT** is a computational project designed to assist in the processing, analysis, and visualization of results from electronic structure calculations, particularly those generated by CASTEP.

Its functionalities include:

- Electronic band structure visualization.
- Density of States (DOS) and Projected Density of States (PDOS).
- Charge density analysis.
- Computational results processing.
- Support for materials and defect investigations.

**Project links:**

- [Official website](https://brunohdmacedo.tech/bruma/)
- [Tutorial and documentation](https://brunohdmacedo.tech/bruma/tutorial.html)
- [Source code — GitHub](https://github.com/brunohdmacedo/bruma-dft)
