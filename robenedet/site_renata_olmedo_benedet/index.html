Competencies & Expertise

Multidisciplinary Scientific Focus

Integrating engineering methodology with physical modeling for clinical and analytical diagnostics.

X-Ray Scattering & Imaging (CS-XSI)

Characterization of healthy and pathological soft tissue using conical slit geometries. Special emphasis on signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) optimization with tissue-equivalent phantoms.

  • Monte Carlo Sim.
  • Radiological Physics
  • Tissue Phantoms

Additive Manufacturing for Precision Optics

Full cycle CAD modeling, slicing optimization, and 3D printing of conical slits and custom optical holders tailored for laboratory and benchtop X-ray beamlines.

  • CAD / CAM
  • FDM & SLA Prototyping
  • Geometric Collimation

Biocrystallography & Structural Analysis

Diffraction pattern evaluation of calcium oxalate dihydrate and microcalcifications. Specialized training in single particle cryo-electron microscopy (Cryo-EM) by the ABCR.

  • Powder XRD
  • Cryo-EM Fundamentals
  • Biological Salts
